# 📻 Zero Radio - User Manual

Welcome to **Zero Radio**, your clean, powerful, and ad-free gateway to thousands of internet radio stations. This guide will help you get the most out of the app’s features.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Adding Your First Stations
There are three ways to populate your radio list:
1.  **Online Search:** Tap the `Menu (⋮)` -> `Add Station (Search)`. Enter a name, country, or genre to browse thousands of stations powered by *Radio Browser*.
2.  **Presets by Country:** Tap `Menu (⋮)` -> `Stations by Country`. Select a country to see a curated list of popular local stations.
3.  **Manual Entry:** Have a specific stream URL? Use `Add Station (Manual)` to paste the link and add a custom logo.

### Organizing Your List
*   **Favorites:** Tap the ❤️ icon on the player bar to favorite a station. You can filter your main view to show *only* favorites using the heart icon in the top bar.
*   **Reordering:** Go to `Settings` -> `Reorder Stations`. Long-press the `⠿` handle and drag stations to your preferred order.
*   **Editing/Deleting:** **Long-press** any station tile on the main screen to open the Edit menu. Here you can change the name, URL, or logo. You can also upload your own logo if you cannot find a good URL.

## 🔊 Playback & Streaming

### Sonos Support
Zero Radio features full Sonos integration:
1.  Ensure your phone and Sonos speakers are on the **same Wi-Fi network**.
2.  Tap the **Cast icon** (Speaker/Devices) in the playback bar.
3.  Select your speaker. The stream will now play directly on your Sonos system.
4.  Use the volume slider that appears to control the Sonos volume.

### Sleep Timer
Tap `Menu (⋮)` -> `Set Sleep Timer`. Choose a duration (15–90 mins). The app will automatically stop playback when the time is up, helping you fall asleep to your favorite music.

### Track History
Ever heard a great song but missed the name? Tap `Menu (⋮)` -> `Track History` to see a list of songs played today across all stations.

## ⏰ Alarm Clock

Zero Radio includes a built-in alarm clock designed for reliability.
*   **Station Selection:** Choose a specific station to wake up to, or select "Last Played" to use whatever you were listening to last.
*   **Reliability (Fallback Sound):** If your internet is down when the alarm goes off, the app will play a **Fallback Sound** instead of silence.
*   **Custom Fallback:** You can upload your own MP3 in the Alarm settings to serve as your emergency wake-up tone.

---

## 🎨 Personalization

Go to `Settings` -> `Grid & List Configuration` to change your view:
*   **Grid Mode:** Choose between 1 to 8 columns. (Tip: 3 or 4 looks best on most phones).
*   **List Mode:** A classic vertical list with larger names.
*   **Auto-Play:** Enable "Auto-play on start" in Settings to immediately resume your last station whenever you open the app.

---

## 💾 Backup & Restore

Don't lose your stations when you get a new phone!
*   **Export:** Go to `Settings` -> `Export Stations`. This creates a `.zip` file containing your station list and any custom logos you've uploaded.
*   **Import:** Select `Import Stations` on your new device and pick the `.zip` file. You can choose to "Add to List" or "Clear & Import" to replace everything.

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting

*   **Station won't play:** Check your internet connection. Some streams may be temporarily offline or restricted to certain regions.
*   **Sonos not found:** Disable "Mobile Data" briefly to ensure your phone is strictly using Wi-Fi, and make sure the speaker is powered on.
*   **Logo not appearing:** If a logo fails to load, the app automatically generates a colorful icon with the station's initials.
*   **App is stopped automatically:** Make sure you go the app unlimited battery permissions as otherwise your phone might at some point kill the app when it is ínactive'for long but still consuming battery. Go to your android settings > Apps > Zero Radio > Battery > Set to Unlimited
